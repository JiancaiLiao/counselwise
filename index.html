Read it before you sign it.

Counselwise AI reads your contract and tells you, in plain English, what you are agreeing to. Tenancy agreements, job offers, phone and gym contracts.

What Counselwise does

  1. Reads every clause

    The whole document, not a summary.

  2. Flags the terms that could cost you

    Fees, notice periods, auto-renewals, penalties.

  3. Explains them in plain English

    What each flagged clause means for you.

  4. Drafts the questions to ask

    Ready to send to the landlord, employer or provider.

How it works

  1. Upload the contract (PDF or photo).
  2. Counselwise AI reads it.
  3. You get one report in minutes.

Questions

Which contracts can I check?
  • Tenancy agreements
  • Employment contracts and job offers
  • Freelance and service contracts
  • Phone, broadband and gym contracts
  • Consumer terms and conditions
Is this legal advice?

No. Counselwise is an information service. It does not replace a solicitor, and for anything with serious consequences you should take professional advice.

What happens to my document?

Nothing is stored. Counselwise is currently a research prototype; see About this study.
