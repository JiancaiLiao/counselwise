About this study

Counselwise is a fictitious brand. It was created for a research project on how firms describe artificial intelligence in professional services, run by researchers at Birmingham Business School, University of Birmingham.

What the study involved

The advert you saw and this website are the whole study. There is no Counselwise service, no waitlist and nothing to sign up for. The page exists only so that we can compare how people respond to different ways of describing an AI service.

What we record

No personal data is collected or stored by the researchers. The site has no forms and no accounts. The Meta Pixel on this site records only that an advert was clicked, whether the "Join the waitlist" button was pressed and the answer to the quick check in the waitlist panel, each tagged with the version of the advert that was clicked, and we see these as aggregate counts, not as information about any individual. Meta processes pixel data under its own policy; see Privacy and cookies.

What happens next

Nothing. You can close this page at any time and nothing further will happen. There is no follow-up contact and no record of who visited.
