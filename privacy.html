Privacy and cookies

Counselwise is a research prototype run by researchers at Birmingham Business School, University of Birmingham. This notice explains what the site does and does not do with your data.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The site has no forms, no accounts and no document upload. The researchers do not collect, store or receive any personal data.

The Meta Pixel

This site uses the Meta Pixel so that we can measure, in aggregate, how many people who clicked one of our adverts visited the page and how many pressed the "Join the waitlist" button. The pixel sets a cookie in your browser and sends event data to Meta. We see this data only as counts and cannot identify individuals from it.

Meta processes this data under its own data policy, which you can read at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy. You can limit the pixel by blocking third-party cookies in your browser or through your Meta ad settings.

Session storage

The page keeps one short value in your browser's session storage (which version of the advert brought you here) so that the counts above can be grouped correctly. It is deleted when you close the browser tab and is never sent to the researchers.
